Why come now

Most people visit Goa chasing the sun.

But when the clouds gather over the coast and the first rain touches the red earth, a different kind of holiday begins. The light softens. The palms grow restless in the wind. Village lanes glisten beneath a canopy of green.

The Goa you thought you knew feels different — not because there is more to do, but because there is suddenly less reason to hurry.

The stay

Fourteen rooms, wooden louvers, a covered porch each.

Every room opens to weather you can watch from a dry chair — a deep veranda, a reading lamp, linen sheets and shutters you can throw open when the shower passes.

Downstairs, amber light falls across the common lounge. A little jazz plays beneath the sound of rain, and there is nothing urgent enough to interrupt the evening.
